Introduction In this reflection blog, I will describe what we learned in our lab sessions.

Lab1: Wiki and Markdown

In the first lab we learned about Wiki and Markdown. The markdown tutorial sheet was given and we practiced writing in Markdown using exercises from that sheet. We also practiced writing content using Markdown such as headings, links etc.Then we installed zettler so that we can do editing in Markdown. Then we wrote few mathematical expressions using Latex format in zettler.Overall,this exercise helped us to know how content can be formatted using Markdown and how wiki pages store and share information and how zettler can be used for editing in Markdown.

Lab2: Wiki Practice

In this lab session we installed hugo and Git then we created a learning resource based on The Information:A History, a Theory, a flood by James Glieck. Each groups were assigned 1 or 2 chapters from this book and groups need to summarize these chapters in 150-200 words and create the resouce on outline.The summary included what the chapter is about and what the author is saying.On outline we also wrote our comments and observations regarding those chapters and addressed the important points in the chapters,new/ surprising thing we found and how our views on nature or history of information got affected by these chapters.
