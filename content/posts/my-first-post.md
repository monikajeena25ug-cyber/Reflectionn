**UNIT 3: REFELCTING ON FAILURE**

The unit 'Reflection on Failure' explained the concept of failure through real life incidents such as Post Office Horizon Scandal and Torrey Cannon Ship incident. Earlier, I used to think that the problems occur just because of one main reason but going through these case studies helped me realise that the 
basic failures are caused by single mistakes while complex failures are not just because of one reason instead various small problems combine together and creates a major failure. 

Our group was assigned the 'Post Office Scandal Horizon' case study which reflected that how sytem failures or technological flaws affetced life of many innocent subpostmaster and were blamed for money shortfalls. It was very surprising that they suffered not because of their own mistakes but because of the system failure which clearly reflected organisation failure as well.

Another case study, the chapter  'The Perfect Storm' also explained many interersting ideas which changed my mindset completely. Using the example of Torrey Canyon incident in which the ship collided with rocks not because of single reason but a combination of time pressure, the missing Channel Pilot and risky decision he took which led to massive oil spill, this chapter explains that failures do not happen in new or risky places but in normal, daily situations as well with combination of small problems.

Overall, the unit was very interesting which made me learn totally different aspect of failure. I leraned that failure does not always impose negative consequences. If these failure are taken seriously and analyzed deeply, they can be a learning experience by addressing even a small problem rather than ignoring these small warning signs.
